# Pitch playbook

One pattern, five channels. Every pitch is four beats, in this order, and never over ~150 words.

| Beat | What it does | Your rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | Prove you read *their* thing | Their name, their product, the exact pain, what it costs them. One or two sentences. |
| 2. Plan | How you would solve it | Two or three concrete steps for *their* problem. No skill list, no bio. |
| 3. Proof | Why you | One shipped thing that looks like their problem, with a link. Numbers if you have them. |
| 4. Close | Lead the next step | Propose an action and a date. Never "hope to hear from you". |

Why the sample letter you pasted loses: it opens with "I'm excited about the opportunity", lists a résumé, never names a specific problem on 8rainStation, and closes passively. Every line could be sent to any company. Yours should only make sense for the one you are sending it to.

## Fill-in slots

`[Name]` first name from reviews, the post, or LinkedIn
`[product]` their app, site or company
`[pain]` the exact problem in their words
`[cost]` what the pain is costing them (time, users, revenue, a launch date)
`[step 1/2/3]` your plan for *that* pain
`[proof]` one item from the bank above
`[date]` a real day this week

## A. Job post or gig (Upwork, Contra, job boards)

> Hi [Name],
>
> You're looking for [pain] on [product]. I looked at [thing you checked], and [one specific observation that shows you looked].
>
> I'd start by [step 1], then [step 2], and ship [step 3] so you have something live within [timeframe].
>
> Closest thing I've done: [proof, one sentence with number]. [link]
>
> If that's the shape of help you want, I can start [date]. Happy to send a short plan first if you'd rather see it on paper.
>
> Stephen

~110 words. Replace the bracketed parts and nothing else.

## B. Cold email (unsolicited)

Subject: `[product] + [outcome in 3 to 5 words]` (e.g. "Hauliin + faster driver onboarding")

> Hi [Name],
>
> [One sentence showing you used or studied the product and found a specific gap.] That's probably costing you [cost].
>
> I'd fix it by [step 1] and [step 2]. I did the same for [proof short], [link].
>
> Worth a 20-minute call [date]? If not, I can send a one-page outline instead.
>
> Stephen
> feranmiadedokun.com

~70 words. Cold mail earns less attention than a reply to a post, so it is shorter.

## C. Intro page (feranmiadedokun.com/intro/<slug>)

Headline: the offer, under 100 characters, three lines max at XXL. Lead with their outcome, not your name.

Patterns that work:
- "[product] is 70% done. **I finish products.** One week, $800, one real piece shipped."
- "**Ship [product] to both stores** without hiring a mobile team."
- "Your agents move ₦100m a day. **Your admin should feel like it.**"

Sub-copy: one sentence pointing at the proof below.

Sections, in order: headline → their explicit asks answered one by one → your plan → one or two works → contact (Calendly, email, résumé). Compose each page for its job; don't reuse the same block order twice in a row.

## D. Application / cover note (company roles)

> Hi [Name],
>
> [Company] is [what they are doing, in their words], and the [team/role] post asks for [top two asks]. I've shipped both: [proof 1 short, link] and [proof 2 short, link].
>
> On [specific thing you noticed about their product], I'd [step 1] first. [One sentence on how you work: small PRs, ship weekly, own it end to end.]
>
> I'm available from [date]. Portfolio: feranmiadedokun.com. Happy to do a short paid trial if that's easier than interviews.
>
> Stephen

~100 words. Two proofs are allowed here because job posts list multiple requirements; still no more than two.

## E. LinkedIn / Twitter DM

> Hi [Name], saw [post/product]. [One specific observation.] I shipped [proof short] last [month], [link]. If [outcome] is on your list, I can start [date]. Call or outline, your pick.

Three sentences. Stop there.

## Checklist before sending

- [ ] Their name and product appear in the first line
- [ ] One sentence proves you looked (a URL, a screen, a review, a number from their post)
- [ ] The plan is about their problem, not your stack
- [ ] Exactly one proof link (two for company applications)
- [ ] The close names an action and a date
- [ ] Under 150 words. If it isn't, cut the bio first, then adjectives
- [ ] Zero of: "excited about the opportunity", "I have X years of experience", "hope to hear from you", "let me know if interested"
